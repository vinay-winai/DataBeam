DataBeam v0.7.0 improves preparation progress and interrupted Sendme transfers.

- Show total preparation progress, completed bytes, and ETA for Croc, Sendme, and EasySendMe. Croc reveals its share code when preparation is ready.
- Smooth speed and ETA over a one-second rolling window while keeping byte progress live.
- Preserve partial Sendme downloads when stopping and retrying, and show the sender's resumed progress correctly.
- Stop Sendme preparation promptly when cancelled and release its temporary resources.
- Update iroh to 1.4.1 and synchronize upstream Sendme improvements while retaining DataBeam's transfer fixes.
- Use a version-free Windows executable name: `databeam_windows-x64.exe`.

Windows validation: 116 app tests, 30 native Sendme tests, Croc engine tests, live Croc/EasySendMe transfers, Sendme Stop/resume checks, and 5 GiB Croc preparation.

**Linux has not been tested on a Linux device.** macOS builds are provided; these changes have not been tested on a Mac device.
