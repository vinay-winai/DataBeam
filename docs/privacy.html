Privacy Policy

Applies to DataBeam (Desktop and Android: com.vinaywinai.databeam) · Effective November 25, 2026

DataBeam is an open-source, peer-to-peer file and text transfer application built around Croc and Sendme. We do not require accounts, show advertisements, or sell or share your personal data.

1. Files, Text, and Transfers

  • End-to-end encrypted transfers: Files, folders, and text snippets you choose to send or receive are transferred directly between your devices over your local network or routed via encrypted Croc or Iroh relays using your transfer code or ticket.
  • No developer servers: DataBeam does not upload your transferred files or text to developer-owned servers.
  • Public or custom relays: When direct peer-to-peer connections cannot be established, encrypted transfer traffic passes through public Croc/Iroh relays or a custom relay you configure in Settings.

2. Local Device Storage

Preferences, recent short codes, receive history, and incomplete transfer caches are stored locally on your device solely to support pause/resume and user preferences. On Android, automatic cloud backup and device-to-device data extraction are disabled.

3. Permissions Used on Android

  • Network & Wi-Fi state: Used to establish direct LAN and relay-assisted peer transfers.
  • Camera (optional): Requested only when you open the built-in QR scanner to read a transfer code or ticket. Camera frames are processed on-device and are not uploaded by DataBeam.
  • Notifications & Foreground Service (dataSync): Used to display live transfer progress and keep user-initiated transfers running reliably in the background.

4. Third-Party Libraries & Network Checks

  • No Analytics or Telemetry SDKs: DataBeam does not include advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs. QR code scanning uses an offline, open-source on-device library (ZXing) with Android CameraX; no images, codes, or scanning diagnostics leave your device.
  • Troubleshooting Guide Check: DataBeam periodically fetches the public troubleshoot.md file from GitHub to display an unread indicator when troubleshooting documentation is updated.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

DataBeam does not retain user files, transfer contents, or personal data on developer servers. Transfer settings, recent short codes, receive history, and incomplete transfer caches remain strictly on your device. You can delete local history and incomplete caches at any time using Settings → Files and storage → Review and clean transfer storage, configure automatic cleanup via Auto-clear history and partials, or remove all local app data by uninstalling the app.

6. Children's Privacy

DataBeam does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes & Contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. If you have privacy questions or support requests, contact goodslothplay@gmail.com or open an issue on the DataBeam GitHub repository.
